North Goa Sightseeing Cab Tour Highlights

The North Goa route typically covers the most famous and energetic spots in the state. Popular stops include:

  • Aguada Fort & Lighthouse
  • Calangute & Baga Beaches
  • Anjuna Beach (Flea Market area)
  • Vagator Beach & Chapora Fort
  • Snow Park / Splashdown Waterpark

South Goa Sightseeing Cab Tour Highlights

For a dose of Goan heritage, culture, and serene beaches, South Goa is unmatched. A typical South Goa taxi package covers:

  • Old Goa Churches (Basilica of Bom Jesus & Se Cathedral)
  • Mangueshi Temple
  • Spice Plantations in Ponda
  • Colva or Palolem Beach
  • Dona Paula Viewpoint & Miramar Beach
  • Mandovi River Cruise Point in Panjim
