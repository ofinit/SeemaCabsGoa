Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand plans change. Here's everything you need to know about cancellations and refunds.

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

At Seema Cabs Goa, we strive to offer flexibility while ensuring our drivers and operations are managed fairly. Please read this policy carefully before making a booking.

Cancel 1+ Hour Before Pickup

Full refund of booking amount will be processed.

Cancel Less Than 1 Hour Before

No refund will be issued. Full booking amount is forfeited.

1. Cancellation Window

Free Cancellation Period

You may cancel your booking at any time up to 1 hour before the scheduled pickup time and receive a full refund of the booking amount paid.

No Refund Zone

Cancellations made less than 1 hour before the scheduled pickup time are non-refundable. The full booking amount will be forfeited.

2. Cancellation Timeline at a Glance

More than 1 hour before pickup ✅ Cancellation allowed — Full refund processed within 5–7 business days.
Less than 1 hour before pickup ❌ No cancellation refund — Booking amount forfeited.
No-show (passenger absent at pickup) ❌ Treated as a late cancellation — No refund issued.

3. How to Cancel a Booking

You can cancel your booking through any of the following methods:

  • Via the Seema Cabs mobile app — Go to My Bookings > Select Booking > Cancel.
  • By calling our support team at +91 97650 40490 or +91 93222 66402.
  • By messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 97650 40490.

Please ensure you receive a cancellation confirmation message before assuming your booking is cancelled.

4. Refund Processing

  • Eligible refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash payments will be refunded via UPI or bank transfer — please provide your account details at the time of cancellation.
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by your bank or payment provider.

5. Company-Initiated Cancellations

In rare situations, we may need to cancel a confirmed booking due to:

  • Unavailability of a driver in your area.
  • Severe weather, road closures, or force majeure events.
  • Vehicle breakdown or safety concerns.

In such cases, a full refund will be issued regardless of when the cancellation occurs, and our team will proactively notify you as soon as possible.

6. Modified Bookings

  • Changes to pickup time, drop location, or vehicle type are subject to availability and may result in fare adjustments.
  • If a modification results in a lower fare, the difference will be refunded as per standard refund timelines.
  • Modifications requested less than 1 hour before pickup will be accommodated where possible but are not guaranteed.

7. Exceptional Circumstances

We handle exceptional situations on a case-by-case basis. If you need to cancel due to a medical emergency or unforeseen crisis, please contact our support team with relevant documentation, and we will do our best to assist you.
